---
title: Tiger habitat along river forests
expected_label: tiger
---
Tigers use floodplain forest, tall grass, and the edges of rivers where ungulates drink. Shade and water matter in hot months. Cubs stay with the female for about two years.

Stripes differ from animal to animal. A male's range may overlap several females. Camera traps record passage on trails and dry watercourses.

Loss of contiguous forest isolates populations even when a reserve looks green on a map.
