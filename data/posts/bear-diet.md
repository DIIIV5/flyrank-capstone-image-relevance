---
title: Bear diet through the seasons
expected_label: bear
---
A brown bear's diet shifts with what the land offers. Spring brings new growth and winter-killed carcasses. Summer adds insects and green plants. Autumn is the fattening period.

Salmon runs, where they occur, concentrate many animals on a short stretch of river. Elsewhere the same species lives almost entirely on plants and small animals.

Hibernation follows a drop in food, not a calendar date.
