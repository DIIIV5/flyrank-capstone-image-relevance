---
title: Deer browsing in winter yards
expected_label: deer
---
In deep snow, white-tailed deer gather in yards where cedar and other browse stay above the crust. Trails are packed by many hooves. Food is twigs, not grass under ice.

Energy is scarce. Animals lose fat through the winter and recover on spring green-up. Antler growth in bucks starts after the old set is shed.

Yard habitat can be a small cedar swamp used by the same group every year.
