---
title: Dog breeds kept for herding
expected_label: dog
---
Herding dogs move livestock at a distance by stance, stare, and short rushes. Collies, heelers, and similar breeds were selected for this work on pasture.

Training starts with a strong eye and a stop whistle. The animal works under a handler rather than killing prey. Rest between runs is part of the day.

These dogs live in kennels or in the house when the flock is in.
