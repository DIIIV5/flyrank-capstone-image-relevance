---
title: Coral bleaching on tropical reefs
---
When sea temperatures stay high for weeks, the algae that live inside coral leave the skeleton. The reef turns white and then starves if the algae do not return.

Bleaching is a response to heat, light, and poor water quality. Recovery depends on how long the stress lasts and whether living coral remains nearby to resettle the dead patches.

Nothing in this article is about terrestrial mammals.
