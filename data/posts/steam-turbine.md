---
title: Steam turbine design in power plants
---
A steam turbine converts pressurised steam into shaft work. Blades extract energy as the steam expands through successive stages. The rotor drives a generator.

Inlet temperature and pressure set the efficiency. Condensers drop the exhaust pressure so more work can be taken from the same mass of steam. Blade deposits from poor water chemistry cut output.

Nothing in this article is about terrestrial mammals or farm animals.
