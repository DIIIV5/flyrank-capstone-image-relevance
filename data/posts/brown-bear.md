---
title: Brown bear foraging in autumn
expected_label: bear
---
Ursus arctos puts on fat before denning. In autumn it eats berries, roots, insects, and fish where streams still hold a run. The walk is a plantigrade shuffle with long claws for digging.

Cubs stay with the female through at least one winter. Adults are usually solitary. A good berry year can mean a shorter time in the den.

The animal is a large omnivore of forest and tundra, not a grazing herd beast of open pasture.
