---
title: The domestic cat in houses
expected_label: cat
---
Felis catus shares rooms, beds, and windowsills with people. It hunts mice when they are present and sleeps for much of the day.

The coat may be short or long. Whiskers and a flexible spine help it pass through small gaps. Scratching posts wear the claws and mark the territory of the house.

A house cat is not a wild felid of open grassland. It is a small companion that still stalks moths and string.
