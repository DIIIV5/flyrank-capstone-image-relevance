---
title: Chicken flock behaviour in yards
expected_label: chicken
---
Gallus gallus domesticus lives in coops and yards. The flock has a peck order. Birds scratch for seeds and insects and dust-bathe in dry soil.

Hens lay in nest boxes. A rooster, if present, warns of hawks. Night is spent on perches off the ground.

These are farm birds kept for eggs or meat, not wild waterbirds or forest mammals.
