---
title: Fox range across continents
expected_label: fox
---
Vulpes vulpes occupies a wide band of the northern hemisphere. It uses woodland, farmland, and the edges of towns, and it shifts dens as food and cover change through the year.

The coat is typically rusty on the back with a white chest. Surplus kills are cached and retrieved later. In winter the animal travels farther and uses dens less often except when rearing cubs.

The skull is narrower than that of a wolf and the ears are larger relative to the head.
