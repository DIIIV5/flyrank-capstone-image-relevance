---
title: The domestic dog as a companion
expected_label: dog
---
Canis familiaris lives with people in houses, yards, and working farms. It is fed by its owners and follows human routines more closely than any wild canid.

Breeds differ in size and coat, but they share a willingness to attend to human cues. Many animals are kept for company rather than for a job.

The species is not a wild hunter of the forest; it is a commensal of people.
