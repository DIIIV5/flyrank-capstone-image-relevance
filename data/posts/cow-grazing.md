---
title: Cow grazing on mixed grass
expected_label: cow
---
Cattle take grass with a sweep of the tongue and graze down a sward more evenly than sheep. Mixed pasture of grass and clover holds protein through the growing season.

The herd rests and chews cud in the heat of the day. Fencing and water troughs set the pattern of movement more than any wild range.

Stocking rate is a farm decision. Too many animals on wet soil poach the surface and cut yield later.
