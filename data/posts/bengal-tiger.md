---
title: Bengal tiger range in South Asia
expected_label: tiger
---
Panthera tigris tigris holds remaining forest and grassland in India, Nepal, Bhutan, and Bangladesh. Adults are solitary except when a female is rearing cubs.

The coat is orange with dark stripes that break up the outline in tall grass. Males patrol large territories marked with scent and scrapes. Prey includes deer and wild pig.

The species is a large striped felid, not a small house animal.
