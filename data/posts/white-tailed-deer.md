---
title: White tailed deer in woodland edges
expected_label: deer
---
Odocoileus virginianus uses the edge between woods and fields. It browses twigs and forbs and feeds on crops where farms meet cover. The tail flashes white when the animal bounds away.

Bucks grow antlers and drop them each year. Does hide fawns in grass for the first weeks. Dawn and dusk are the main feeding times.

The species is a medium ungulate of eastern woods and farmland, not a predator.
