---
title: Grey wolves of the northern forests
expected_label: wolf
---
Canis lupus is a large social canid of boreal forest, tundra, and mountain country. Packs hunt ungulates and scavenge when herds move on.

Adults are typically grey or tawny with a thick winter coat. Howls hold the pack together over long distances. Pups are raised in a den and then at rendezvous sites as they grow.

The gait is heavier than that of a red fox, and the muzzle is broader.
