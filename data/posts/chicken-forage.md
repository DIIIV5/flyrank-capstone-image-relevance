---
title: Chicken forage and dust baths
expected_label: chicken
---
Free-range chickens work a patch of ground for grit, seeds, and insects. Dust bathing keeps feathers in order and reduces mites. Waterers and a coop door structure the day.

Forage quality depends on cover. Bare dirt yields less than a mixed yard with weeds and leaf litter. Grit in the gizzard grinds grain.

A laying hen returns to the same nest box once she has chosen it.
