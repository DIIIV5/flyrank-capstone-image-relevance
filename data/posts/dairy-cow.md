---
title: Dairy cow husbandry on pasture
expected_label: cow
---
Bos taurus kept for milk graze ryegrass and clover and return to a parlour twice a day. Rumination breaks down fibre. Water and shade matter in warm weather.

Calves are reared separately on many farms. The adult is selected for yield and udder health, not for speed. Hooves need trimming on wet ground.

A dairy herd is a managed grazer, not a wild forest animal.
