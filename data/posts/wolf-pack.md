---
title: Wolf pack hunting in winter
expected_label: wolf
---
A pack of Canis lupus tests herds of deer and elk through deep snow. Weaker animals are isolated and run down. Meat is shared at the kill and later at rest sites.

Winter coats are dense. Tracks in snow show a direct register walk. Howling after a hunt keeps members of the pack in contact when they scatter to rest.

The hunt is a group effort, not a solitary ambush.
