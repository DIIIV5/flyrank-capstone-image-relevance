---
title: The behaviour of red foxes
expected_label: fox
---
Vulpes vulpes is the most widely distributed wild canid of its genus. It lives in woodland, farmland, and the edges of towns, and it hunts small mammals, birds, insects, and fruit.

Adults are typically rusty-red on the back with a white chest and a tail tipped in white. They cache surplus food and return to it later. In winter they travel farther and use dens less often, except when rearing cubs.

The species is often confused with wolves and large dogs in photographs. The skull is narrower, the ears are larger relative to the head, and the gait is lighter than that of a wolf.
