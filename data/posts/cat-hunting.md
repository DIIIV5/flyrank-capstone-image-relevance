---
title: Cat hunting at dusk
expected_label: cat
---
A domestic cat waits at the edge of a garden as light falls. It uses hearing and sight to find small mammals and birds, then a short pounce.

The tail balances the leap. Whiskers gauge gaps. After a kill the animal often carries the prey back toward the house rather than eating it in the open.

Night hunting is common even when bowls of food are filled in the kitchen.
